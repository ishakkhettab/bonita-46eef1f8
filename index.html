For Meriem, Bonita

A little message for a long night

Hey, bonita.

I know you didn't sleep well, and I know that makes everything feel heavier today. You're allowed to be grumpy. You're allowed to be tired. You don't have to be okay for anyone right now, not even for me.

Just know this: even on your worst-slept, messiest-hair, don't-talk-to-me days, you are still the most beautiful thing in my day. Your bad mood doesn't scare me, bonita. I'll take every version of you.

Drink some water. Eat something small. Close your eyes for ten minutes if you can. And if the day gets loud, come back to this page and remember that someone is thinking about you nonstop.

Tap the button. Papi left you some things to say.

All yours, Papi ♡
